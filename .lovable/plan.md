# Privacy note on feedback

## What's true today
- Session ratings and Rate the day answers are saved with the person's account so tickets can be counted. The moderator desk shows feedback without names or emails.
- Q&A questions are linked to the submitter for organizers. Other attendees see them anonymously.
- Ticket totals are shown per attendee in the moderator Raffle tab.

So the accurate wording is "not shown with your name", not "fully anonymous".
